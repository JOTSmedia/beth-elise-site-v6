## Resolution and limits

Main imagery and logo delivery exports have approximately 4096-pixel long edges or larger. These are regenerated or source-preserving upscales, **not native 4K/8K captures**. Built-in image generation returned approximately 1K–1.7K source images. Avatar rig layers and detailed branded product variants were enlarged conservatively to preserve alignment and print. Small UI icons remain their original mapped assets; browser icons were rebuilt for their actual display sizes. No 8K detail is claimed.

All imagery was produced with the built-in image generation tool; no paid API fallback was used. Exact prompts, generated source sizes, delivery sizes, hashes and exceptions are in the art reports. The original portrait was preserved and upscaled, without inventing facial features. Existing hat/hoodie overlay mockups and several tinted product variants remain source-preserving upscales.
