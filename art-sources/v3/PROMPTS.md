Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: circular iris texture for an animated eye, isolated on transparent background. Direct front-on extreme macro photograph of one circular realistic human iris with a centered round black pupil approximately 30 percent of the iris diameter. Rich subtle amethyst violet, lavender-grey and dark plum radial stromal fibers, intricate irregular crypts, realistic collarette, dark charcoal limbal ring, softly glossy transparent cornea with one soft rectangular photographic catchlight at upper left. Violet color should remain believable and detailed, never fluorescent. Perfectly circular outer silhouette, full iris centered in a square canvas occupying 90 percent of width, true transparent outside circle, no sclera, no eyelids, no skin, no other objects, no stars, no galaxy, no symbols, no drawing or cartoon, no text.

Generated using built-in ImageGen. The existing blue morpho is retained from v1/v2. Each new species has its native PNG, 4096-pixel delivery master and a 2048-pixel transparent WebP used by the site.
